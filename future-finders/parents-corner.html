Parents’ Corner.

Thank you for helping young people explore their curiosity safely. Future Finders is an early-stage project about identifying real-world problems and developing creative, inclusive solutions.

What is the project trying to do?

Future Finders hopes to help people turn observations and ideas into thoughtful problem-solving. It may involve brainstorming, researching existing solutions, making designs, and learning from different perspectives. It is still being developed; it is not currently a formal programme with open recruitment.

🛡️ Safety comes first

Before inviting submissions or team members, the project needs clear safeguarding procedures, suitable adult oversight, and a safe way to manage communications.

🔐 Privacy

🔐 Privacy matters. Please share only the information a form specifically asks for, and never include passwords, exact home addresses, or sensitive personal details. Only submit information when a form is available and clearly confirms that your submission has been sent. A trusted adult can help review how information is collected and used.

👨‍👩‍👧 Adult involvement

A trusted adult should help review website services, privacy terms, contact methods, moderation, and any legal or financial decisions before the project accepts submissions or applications.

📣 Honest expectations

Ideas may be interesting without being practical or original. The project should research carefully and never promise that every submission will be developed or receive a personal reply.

Before the project opens

We aim to handle information responsibly. Before sharing personal details or joining activities, families should review the information requested, check how it will be used, and involve a trusted adult. Please do not assume a form has been submitted unless the website confirms it.
