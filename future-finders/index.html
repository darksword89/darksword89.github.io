💌 The Future Finders Advice Corner

Kind advice for tricky moments

Questions, worries, friendship problems or big ideas? Start with a small step, be kind to yourself, and remember that asking for help is a strength.

💡

“What if my idea sounds silly?”

Ideas don’t have to be perfect to be worth exploring. Write down the problem, think of one small step, and ask someone you trust to help you explore it. Brilliant ideas often begin with a question.

💚

“Someone is being left out. What can I do?”

You could invite them to join in, listen kindly, or check how they’re feeling. If someone is being bullied or treated unfairly, tell a trusted adult. You don’t have to handle it alone.

🌱

“The problem feels too big. Where do I start?”

Pick one small, safe thing you can do. Ask what would make things a little easier, then get help from a trusted adult when the problem affects someone’s safety or wellbeing.
