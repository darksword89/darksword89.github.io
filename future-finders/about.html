Why Future Finders exists

Big problems. Brilliant ideas.

Future Finders is a growing project built around one simple belief: people of all ages can notice problems and imagine creative ways to make life better.

What is Future Finders?

Future Finders aims to explore real-world problems and develop thoughtful, practical ideas that could help solve them. We want to listen to different perspectives, especially from people who experience a problem first-hand, and make room for ideas from people with different needs and abilities.

🔎 Find problems

Notice everyday things that are difficult, unfair, confusing, inaccessible, or could work better.

💡 Imagine solutions

Think creatively, ask questions, and turn a big challenge into smaller steps we can explore.

🌍 Make a difference

Research what already exists, learn from others, and work towards solutions that are useful and inclusive.

Our promise

We want to treat people’s ideas respectfully, be honest about what we can and cannot do, and protect people’s privacy. Future Finders is still developing, so we won’t claim an idea has been tested or solved until that is actually true.
